2.  **Enhanced Business Registration:** Country selection, Google Maps location picking.

5.  **AI Dynamic Pricing & Demand Prediction:** Real-time pricing, smart pricing, predictive analytics.
6.  **Computer Vision & AR Features:** Receipt scanning, AR store locator, visual search.
7.  **Enterprise-Grade Security (4-phases):** API Security (rate limiting, input validation), Enhanced Authentication (MFA, JWT, RBAC), Data Protection (GDPR, encryption), Mobile Security (device protection, integrity checks).
8.  **Modern Design & Branding:** UI/UX overhaul, DealWiz brand, diamond logo, contemporary aesthetics.
The app aims to be a market leader by offering intelligent, secure, and visually appealing features.
