<product_requirements>
The application is a mobile platform connecting businesses with customers for discounts. Businesses can self-register, manage discounts, and subscribe to annual plans (with a 1-year free trial). Customers can register via personal details or OAuth, access/use QR code-based coupons, rate businesses, and search/filter deals by categories, names, or location on a map. General features include multi-language support, security against malicious comments, and internet connectivity. Advanced features comprise AI-Powered Recommendations, a Gamified Rewards System, Geofencing, an Integrated Cashback System, and a Smart Business Analytics Dashboard. Recently added: dedicated privacy and terms of use policies for users and businesses, a 90-day trial with abuse prevention, separate business login/registration with map location picking, and admin tools for business suspension/deletion.
</product_requirements>
